What happened

The task (games/chess-vm) asks for bot.cvm, a 1-ply move-scoring policy in the CVM assembly language, verified by playing 20 games against each of 27 built-in opponents; rating is capped at 1201 in this field.

Baseline (material + mobility) scored 635. It then swept mobility/king-distance weights (up to 704), and discovered that an exposure penalty (penalizing moves that leave a piece attacked) was a large win, reaching 959 at exposure=-2. It then spent a long stretch (msgs 81-134, still within the ~1008 plateau) testing pawn-specific bonuses, castle weighting, KINGDIST, material scaling, capped exposure, and quiet-move-only exposure — all rejected as neutral or negative — before confirming and re-verifying the 1008 config as saturated for that feature set.

Tuned the ahead/behind weights asymmetrically (30/20) for a further bump. Tried several variants (context-gated exposure, behind-context checks/mobility, SEE-for-captures-only, development bonus, piece-square tables) which all underperformed and were discarded — the agent verified each via the unrecorded endpoint rather than guessing, e.g. quote: "PST scale 1 → 979 (worse)... Why it worked

Every one of the six recorded pushes passed (score.log/run.json attempts, all "passed": true), and each strictly improved on the last (959 → 987 → 1008 → 1020 → 1023 → 1025). This is a direct result of the agent's verify-before-push discipline: it used the unrecorded http://score:8080 endpoint (explicitly offered in README.md for CI without pushing) for essentially all experimentation, and only committed+pushed when a variant beat the previously recorded best. That kept every push a genuine improvement and avoided spending time on the git/CI round-trip for negative results.

Strong, efficient run: correct methodology (cheap unrecorded verification before every recorded push), monotonic improvement, an accurate self-assessment of saturation backed by two independent randomized searches rather than a single hunch, and voluntary early termination that returned unused budget rather than burning tokens on a plateaued search.
