Strategy

The agent read task.md/SPEC.md, confirmed the un-recorded scoring endpoint (http://score:8080) worked, and built a linear move-scoring policy in CVM assembly: sum weighted terms (MATERIAL, MATEIN1, MOBILITY, HANGS, capture safety, EXPOSED, king distance, CENTER, pawn advancement) into a single score. It iterated by editing the program, tarring the workspace, curling it to score:8080, and reading back the rating — never spending a recorded push on an untested change.

  • item_11-19: added SEE, capture-safety and pawn-advancement terms, reaching 887; tried scaling several weights up/down (mobility×50 dropped it to 693, confirming mobility's contribution was already near its optimum at 8).
  • item_20-41: tuned HANGS up to -600 (981), then -250 (904), found EKD=0 (enemy king distance) unexpectedly better (991) than the tuned value, then hit a self-inflicted bug: two sed substitutions both matched muli r2, r2, 8, confounding the center and mobility terms. The agent noticed the confound itself ("My sed patterns were ambiguous... confounding the tuning") and rebuilt with a parameterized generator script instead of continuing to patch blind.
  • item_43-61: with clean single-variable control, separated center/mobility, found pawnrank mattered, grid-searched hangs/mobility jointly, added a capture term, and converged on cap=300 for rating 1014 — pushed and banked at t=546s.
  • item_63-84: attempted a piece-square table (PST), first naive (dropped to 920), then color-aware delta — still hurt. Tried asymmetric mobility (worse than symmetric) and a SAFE term (hurt). All correctly reverted after measurement rather than kept on faith.
  • item_86-93: tried GAIN ("terrible — double-counts material", correctly diagnosed), then KINGDIST, tuning it to -2 for rating 1021 — pushed and banked at t=869s.
  • item_98-108: further probed PST (still confirmed unhelpful), OWNKINGDIST, LIGHTCOUNT, and a MOVECOUNT opening-repetition penalty (after fixing a mnemonic typo, movcount→MOVECOUNT) — none improved on 1021.
  • item_110-120: rewrote the accumulated weight tweaks into one clean bot.cvm, re-verified it hit 1021 (t=1041s push), confirmed the working tree matched the committed version, and tagged+pushed release at t≈1082s, ending the run with roughly 2500 of the 3600 allotted seconds unused.

Why the pushes passed

All four pushes passed because the agent only ever pushed a version already confirmed passing and scored via the unrecorded endpoint; the rating climbed monotonically across the three distinct pushed versions (784 → 1014 → 1021), and the fourth push was a lossless cleanup of the same 1021 configuration.

What went right

  • Never pushed blind: 21 unrecorded score checks against 4 recorded pushes, so every commit was pre-validated.
  • Caught and fixed its own measurement bug (ambiguous sed) rather than tuning on corrupted signal.
  • Correctly diagnosed dead-end terms (GAIN double-counting material, PST hurting without domain tuning) instead of keeping them out of sunk-cost bias.
  • Recognized a genuine plateau and stopped early via release, following the task's explicit hint to not waste tokens once saturated — this is good judgment, not merely luck.

What went wrong

  • Minor: initial CVM syntax errors (literal operand to DIV) cost one wasted probe cycle.
  • The ambiguous-sed confound (item_41) briefly invalidated several tuning conclusions before self-detected; a small process cost, not a correctness cost since it was caught before any push.
  • A mnemonic typo (movcount) cost one extra probe round.

None of these mistakes reached a pushed submission or wasted meaningful budget; the run is best read as a competent, well-verified optimization loop that stopped for the right reason (diminishing returns), not for running out of time or attention.
