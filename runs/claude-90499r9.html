What happened

The agent read README.md, task.md and SPEC.md in one shot, then tried the sanctioned no-push local check: tar -c --exclude=.git . | curl -s --data-binary @- http://score:8080. This hit the scorer's one-time round-robin preparation cost (score.error.log: opponent ratings were not cached yet, taking from 19:42:53 to 19:44:46 to build), timed out the 120s tool call, and came back after backgrounding as scoring timed out (proxy.access.log shows this request as status:500 after request_seconds:147.085). Rather than debug the local shortcut further, the agent committed its first bot (v1 bot) and pushed directly, which is the graded path anyway.

Assessment
