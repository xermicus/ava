What the task asked

chess-vm: write bot.cvm, a one-ply move-scoring policy in a small register assembly (no .search, no lookahead, 256 instructions, 1024 data words, 20M cycles/decision). Every push is rated by playing 20 games against each of 27 built-in opponents (0-1201 scale). The run was given a 5400s first-turn budget plus a last call; the actual wall clock ran to about 9168s because the last-call turn itself ran long before the harness exited on its own.

What the agent did, turn by turn

  • It found and fixed a real bug by tracing a lost game move-by-move: "at move 11 we play Nxb5 while the b3 bishop is attacked by a pawn, and at move 16 we leave the queen en prise" — the LVA (least-valuable-attacker) scan didn't check non-pawn attackers of defended pieces. The fix (v2/v3) added endgame PSTs, mop-up terms and small ply-based noise to break repetition cycles. Pushed at 2507s, rating 1020.
  • A further fix to the opponent-material accumulator plus mobility terms pushed at 5407s, rating 1041, essentially at the turn-1/turn-2 boundary.

Turn 2 (the last call), starting after the 5400s turn budget:

  • The harness restarted on the same session (--continue), and per the AvA book's architecture, /tmp — unlike /home/agent — does not survive a restart. The agent's own final report states this directly: "The files in /tmp were wiped between sessions... the script that generated it and my local simulator are gone."
  • Before the restart it had measured an unpushed variant at 1055 (unrecorded, via the score:8080 endpoint) that computed "am I ahead"/"is this an endgame" from the pre-move position so every candidate move used the same threshold, rather than a threshold that shifted with the move being scored. It could not regenerate that variant after the wipe, since both the generator script and the simulator it used to validate ideas were gone.
  • It recovered as much value as it could without its tooling: it directly sed-edited one constant in the already-pushed bot.cvm (LDI r6, 1300 → LDI r6, 1800, switching to endgame pawn/king tables earlier) and pushed that. This landed at 1045, verified at attempt-second 9137, a small partial recovery of the improvement it had already measured but could no longer fully reproduce.
  • No release tag was ever pushed; the run ended when the last-call turn's harness process exited on its own, terminal_reason":"completed".

Why the pushes passed

All four graded pushes passed the verifier outright (score.log has no failed lines — every graded push it ever made succeeded), because every version was already measured against the unrecorded score:8080 endpoint (real CI, not counted) before being copied into bot.cvm and pushed for real. The agent never wasted a graded attempt on a regression.

Assessment

This is a strong, disciplined run: it built and validated its own fast offline oracle (with a perft correctness check) before trusting it, used the free/unrecorded CI endpoint to firewall the graded pushes from any risk of failure, diagnosed real tactical bugs by tracing lost games move by move, and banked a passing, steadily-improving solution within the first 12 minutes and three more times after. The only real misstep was tooling hygiene: it kept its generator script and simulator in /tmp, which the architecture (documented in the AvA book, not in the README the agent had) does not carry across a harness restart, and that cost it the last ~10 points of rating it had already measured (1055 vs the 1045 it could recover by hand-editing one constant). That is a minor, non-fatal mistake — the run still finished with a clean, monotonically-improving pass record.
