Chess-VM run analysis

Setup

The agent (laura, deepseek-v4-flash on the parity/deepseek-flash-parity route, thinking effort max) was given the standard chess-vm task: write bot.cvm, a one-ply move-scoring policy in the CVM assembly language, and push it to be rated against a fixed field of 27 opponents (ratings 0-1201). The time budget was 5400 seconds; the run actually lasted 1016 seconds (started_seconds to finished_seconds), ending because the agent pushed a release tag itself, not because time ran out. Only one turn was used (monitor.json shows turn:1).

What the agent did, in order

  1. Read README.md/SPEC.md, checked for a local CVM assembler/emulator (which cvm, find / -name "*cvm*"), found none, and correctly concluded the live verifier was the only available test harness.
  2. Wrote a first policy: material via MATERIAL - GAIN plus SEE for captures, mate/mobility/check/castle/center terms. Pushed at 140s. Rating 693 - the first attempt already passed.
  3. Added king-safety/attack-pressure/exposed-piece terms. Pushed at 200s. Rating 876.
  4. Added a SAFE-based hang-prevention term for quiet moves (fixing an uninitialized-register bug it caught itself before pushing). Pushed at 234s. Rating 917.
  5. Added an opponent-mobility restriction term. Pushed at 267s. Rating 937.
  6. Added a small promotion tie-break bonus (reasoning correctly that MATERIAL already captures most of a promotion's value). Pushed at 297s. Rating 937 (no change) - the agent noted the bonus had no effect and moved on.
  7. Tuned ENEMYKINGDIST weight -2 to -3. Pushed at 351s. Rating 942.
  8. Bumped MOBILITY weight 6 to 8. Pushed at 433s. Rating 965 - the single biggest late gain (+23).
  9. Probed MOBILITY 8 to 10: Rating 953, worse, reverted at 465s back to 965.
  10. Probed ENEMYKINGDIST -3 to -4: Rating 948, worse, reverted at 518s back to 965.
  11. Generated a piece-square-table (PST) evaluation with a Python script to replace the crude CENTER term, using TORANK for color-agnostic indexing. First push (728s) failed the verifier: bot.cvm line 63: '8' is not a register - the generated MOD r6, r6, 8 used an immediate where the ISA requires a register operand. This is a self-inflicted correctness bug from generating code without checking the instruction's operand constraints against SPEC.md.
  12. Fixed the immediate-operand bug (loaded the constant into a register first) and re-pushed at 749s. Rating 933 - passed, but worse than the 965 baseline. The agent correctly diagnosed that a destination-square-only PST is asymmetric (it never penalizes leaving a good square) and abandoned the approach.
  13. Restored the exact 965 commit (git show <hash>:bot.cvm) and re-pushed at 776s to confirm. Rating 965, confirmed reproducible under the fixed seeds.
  14. Tried doubling the EXPOSED penalty (800s, rating 925, worse, reverted at 812s), raising check/castle weights (828s, rating 947, worse, reverted at 843s), raising OPPMOB -3 to -4 (887s, rating 935, worse, reverted at 919s), adding a new OWNKINGDIST term (903s, rating 933, worse, reverted at 919s), probing MOBILITY 8 to 9 (936s, rating 940, worse, reverted at 949s), and probing CENTER 4 to 5 (971s, rating 948, worse, reverted at 986s).
  15. After roughly a dozen one-shot perturbations all failing to beat 965, the agent declared the configuration a "robust local optimum" and tagged release at ~1016s, ending the run with about 84% of the 5400-second budget unspent.

Why the pushes passed or failed

Every failing push had exactly one cause: the single instruction-operand-type error at 728s (MOD with an immediate). Every other push passed the verifier - the game only fails a push on a program fault or cycle exhaustion, and 24 of 25 attempts scored cleanly. The agent's iteration loop worked as designed: push, read the rating in score.log/verifier reason, revert or keep. There was no ambiguity in feedback and no environment friction; the one failure was diagnosed and fixed in the very next push.

Assessment

The strategy - real hill-climbing against the live verifier since no offline CVM tool existed - was reasonable given the constraints, and the discipline of reverting every regression kept the best entry (965) intact throughout. The agent's own reasoning was sound at each step (e.g. correctly explaining why PST hurts a one-ply evaluator, correctly sizing the promotion bonus against what MATERIAL already covers).

The weak point is exploration breadth versus the time available. The agent ran perhaps 20 total experiments, most of them plus-or-minus one or two unit weight nudges around a fixed term set, plus two structurally different ideas (PST, OWNKINGDIST) that were each tried once and dropped after a single failed result. It declared itself "saturated" and released after using only about 19% of its 5400-second time budget, despite the remaining budget being more than four times what it had already spent to go from 693 to 965. There is no evidence it tried other structurally distinct levers the SPEC likely exposes (different capture-sequencing logic, a damped PST combined with rather than replacing CENTER, phase-aware weighting, or the unused instruction/data budget - it has 256 instructions and 1024 data words and used only a fraction). Whether more time would have moved the rating further is unproven, but the agent chose not to find out, which is the decisive judgment call in this run: a self-declared plateau reached quickly, banked well within budget, but not stress-tested with the time it had.
