A key realization came from studying the opponents: "jordi and pancake both self-destruct. d_loop (turtle) is best. Since pancake only writes upward from its position, a loop parked at address 0 is safe from pancake." That led to a park-at-0 turtle, but the first attempt hit a real tooling bug: "The mov [0],imm encoding was broken. Let me use eax=0 instead." The final i_park0 warrior is:

  xor eax, eax
  mov dword [eax], 0x0000feeb
  jmp 0

which writes eb fe (jmp $ self-loop) to address 0 and parks there. Benchmarks showed "i_park0 is a big improvement: 87% vs pancake, 100% vs jordi" and, in a round-robin against synthetic opponents, 100% vs a replicator, 67% vs an aggressive bomber and draws against a wipe.
